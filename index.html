Honest answers

What people worry about.

I'm really unfit. Will I embarrass myself?

No. Most people start exactly where you are. Your first session is mostly talking and gentle movement. Nobody's watching and nobody's judging.

I've failed at this before. Why would this be different?

Because last time nobody was watching. We fix the actual reason it stalled, no structure and no accountability once motivation dipped, rather than asking you to want it more.

I genuinely don't have time.

Two hours a week. An NHS doctor on 12-hour shifts and a bloke running three businesses both found it. We build around your diary, not the other way round. If the gym's the problem, we come to your home or coach you online.

Do I have to give up the food I love?

No. No banned foods, no meal plan you'll abandon. Mark lost 28kg and still has a beer. We change the smallest number of things that actually move the needle.

I'm in my 50s or 60s. Am I too old?

No. We coach every age and ability, working around dodgy knees, backs and shoulders. Cameron specialises in exactly that. Progress at your pace beats intensity you can't sustain.

What does it cost, and am I tied in?

Online coaching is £120 a month, gym packages start at £160 a month or £500 for a block of ten, and home packages are priced by location, from £60 an hour. We'll find the right package at your free consultation and you'll know the exact number before you commit to anything.

What actually happens at the free consultation?

One hour. We ask what you've tried, what your week looks like and what you want to be able to do, then we train. You ask us anything. Then we both decide. No follow-up spam either way.
